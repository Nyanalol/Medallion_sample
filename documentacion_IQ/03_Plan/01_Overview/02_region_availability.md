Important

This feature is in preview.

## Unsupported regions

Plan (preview) is NOT available in the following regions.

- Austria East

- Belgium Central

- Chile Central

- East US

- East US 2

- India West

- Israel Northwest

- Korea South

- Qatar Central

- South Central US

- UAE Central

- France South

- Germany North

- Japan West

## Supported regions

Plan (preview) is available in all other Fabric regions not listed above, as described in Fabric region availability.
